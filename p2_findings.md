## 10. [hypothesis] MARKETING/WEB - Marketing attribution is compared against online sales base
- Evidence: Attributed revenue averages 584.00% of online net sales by month with coefficient of variation 0.70. The 584% figure is the monthly average of ratios; total attributed revenue divided by total online net sales is 550.86% (5.5x). The gap is a mean-of-ratios effect and both figures exceed online sales, so this is a data/model limitation, not a business insight. All-channel share is context only. No causal claim is made.
- Grain: month
- Denominator: online channel net sales
- Size: 584.00%
- Limitation: Attribution is modeled; correct comparison base is online channel net sales, not all-channel revenue. Attributed revenue exceeds online sales, so shares cannot be used for budget decisions until the attribution method is clarified.

## Definition Notes
- Stockout-days rows mean Stockout_Days_In_Month > 0. Closing-stock-zero rows mean Closing_Stock == 0. Overlap is reported separately in metrics.json and inventory tables.
- Backtest scope is product x month demand. It is run for all locations combined and each location separately. The holdout horizon is the last 3 months. Seasonal naive and 3-month moving average are scored on the same rows where both predictions exist. MAPE excludes zero actuals only.
- Hotspot findings use cells with a minimum sample size and report spread against typical cells rather than headlining only the maximum cell.
